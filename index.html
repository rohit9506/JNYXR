The Founder

Jivi founded JNYXR as a creative rebellion against rigid, mass-produced fast fashion templates.

Focused on draped occasionwear, custom corsetry, and raw structural silhouettes, she brings a highly personal, youth-centered eye to modern dressmaking.

Every piece in the collection is drafted, cut, and fitted in her atelier. For Jivi, fashion is not just apparel; it is an intimate canvas of feelings, memories, and textiles. "every stitch holds a story" is not just a tagline, it is our core work ethic.
